Create a modern, minimal macOS app icon for a utility app called "Focus".

App description: it shrinks a large external monitor down to a smaller working area by
painting black borders around the edges, so you work inside a calm, narrower window of
screen instead of a sprawling 32-inch panel.

Concept: a rounded-square icon. Inside it, a thick solid near-black frame occupying the
outer band, and one smaller rectangle perfectly centered inside that frame — the bright
"focused" working area. The composition should read instantly as "a screen inside a
screen", or a viewfinder.

Style requirements:
- Flat design. No gradients, no 3D, no bevels, no drop shadows, no glassmorphism.
- Single iconic geometric mark, absolutely no text or letters.
- Legible at 1024x1024 AND at 32x32 — only large shapes, no fine detail or thin lines.
- Exactly two colors plus the frame: near-black #0B0B0F for the outer band, and a warm
  amber #FFB020 for the inner focused rectangle.
- Generous padding: the mark must not touch the icon edges.
- Rounded-square (macOS Tahoe superellipse) outer silhouette, filled edge to edge.
- Premium and restrained, in the visual language of Linear, Things 3 and Bartender.
- No generic symbols: no eyes, no targets/bullseyes, no crosshairs, no magnifying glasses,
  no lightbulbs, no gears.

Output: perfectly square, high resolution, PNG.
